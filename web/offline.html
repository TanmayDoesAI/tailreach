Can't reach your Mac

It may be asleep, offline, or this device isn't connected to Tailscale.

  1. Is Tailscale on here?Open the Tailscale app on this device and check it's connected.
  2. Is the Mac awake and online?A sleeping Mac drops off Tailscale. In tailreach's menu-bar icon, keep Keep Mac Reachable on.
  3. Then try again.It can take a few seconds after the Mac wakes.
